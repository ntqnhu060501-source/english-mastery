Build sentences with intention.

SENTENCE PATTERN

It takes + someone + time + to V

Dùng để diễn đạt ai đó cần bao nhiêu thời gian để làm việc gì.

It takes me 30 minutes to get to work.
Tôi mất 30 phút để đến chỗ làm.

Biến thể: It took her two years to become fluent. / How long does it take you to get there?

PARAPHRASING WORKSHOP

One idea. Many expressions.

Rewrite without changing the meaning:

“I don't have enough time to finish this task.”

Possible alternatives:
• I'm short on time, so I can't finish this task.
• I don't have sufficient time to complete this task.
• There's not enough time for me to get this done.
